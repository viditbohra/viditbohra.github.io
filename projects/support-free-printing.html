Support Free 3D Printing

ME230, Prof. Ramesh Singh, IIT Bombay · Jan 2026 to Apr 2026

Printing 90 degree overhangs with no support material, on an unmodified 3-axis printer.

  • Python
  • G-code
  • Non planar slicing
  • FDM
  • SolidWorks

Printing a 90 degree overhang normally means printing supports, then cutting them off and cleaning up the scar they leave behind. This project prints them with no support at all, on an unmodified 3-axis FDM printer with no hardware changes.

All of the work happens in software. The STL is pre warped, sliced with an ordinary planar slicer, and the resulting G-code is put through a back transformation in Python so the nozzle traces conical non planar layers that support themselves as they build. The result is 25% less material, 20% shorter build time, and no post processing at all.
